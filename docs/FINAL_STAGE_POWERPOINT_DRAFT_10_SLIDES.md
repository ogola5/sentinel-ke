# Sentinel-KE Final Stage PowerPoint Draft

Presenter: `Evance Ogola`  
Project: `Sentinel-KE`

Use this as draft slide text for a 10-slide PowerPoint.

The deck is designed for the final-stage rubric:
- cross-sector resilience
- national sovereignty
- scalability and speed to impact
- public trust and ethics
- economic and operational ROI
- visionary innovation

The tone should be:
- national
- operational
- evidence-led
- disciplined
- confident without hype

## Slide 1 — Title / One Sentence

**Title**

`Sentinel-KE: Sovereign digital defense for Kenya`

**Bullets**

- A national workflow for detecting, connecting, explaining, and responding to digital risk
- Built for cyber, fraud, and public-integrity operations
- Designed for agencies to coordinate without surrendering all raw data
- Presented by `Evance Ogola`

**Speaker note**

Sentinel-KE is not just a dashboard and not just an AI model. It is a sovereign operational workflow for Kenya. It helps institutions see related digital risk, explain why it matters, and coordinate safe response across agencies.

---

## Slide 2 — The Problem

**Title**

`Kenya’s digital systems are growing faster than our coordination capacity`

**Bullets**

- Public services, payments, telecoms, and procurement are now deeply digital
- But cyber, fraud, and integrity signals still sit in fragmented systems and separate teams
- Institutions often detect pieces of the problem, not the whole chain
- That creates slower response, weaker trust, higher losses, and national vulnerability

**Speaker note**

The real problem is not lack of data. Kenya already has logs, alerts, transactions, audit trails, and service telemetry. The real problem is fragmentation. Threats move across systems faster than institutions can correlate, explain, and act.

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution

**Title**

`What Sentinel-KE is`

**Bullets**

- A sovereign intelligence workflow for Kenya
- Ingests signals from agencies and existing tools through connectors and event feeds
- Correlates related entities in a graph
- Prioritizes risk with GNNs and ML
- Explains the decision with evidence, then routes bounded response and reporting

**Speaker note**

The simplest explanation is this: Sentinel-KE turns scattered signals into one explainable operational picture. It helps agencies move from detection to correlation to prioritized response without forcing rip-and-replace.

---

## Slide 4 — Cross-Sector Resilience

**Title**

`Why this strengthens more than one sector`

**Bullets**

- Cyber incidents against public services can also involve telco abuse, identity abuse, and payment risk
- Fraud chains like SIM swap and mule movement affect banks, telcos, wallets, and citizens at once
- Procurement and integrity risks affect service delivery, budgets, and national trust
- Sentinel-KE creates resilience spillover by connecting these sectors instead of treating them as silos

**Speaker note**

This project matters nationally because the value is not trapped in one use case. The same workflow helps public services, banks, telecoms, and integrity-sensitive systems. Threats cross sectors, so resilience must also cross sectors.

---

## Slide 5 — Sovereignty and Federation

**Title**

`National sovereignty without forced raw-data centralization`

**Bullets**

- Agencies can connect directly or operate in federated edge mode
- Sensitive raw data can stay inside the agency when needed
- Sentinel-KE can share hashed high-risk patterns upward for cross-agency correlation
- This gives Kenya local control over data, workflow, policy, and AI governance

**Speaker note**

Sovereignty here does not mean isolation. It means Kenya retains control over how digital risk is seen, interpreted, and acted on. That is why federation matters: collaboration without unnecessary exposure of raw agency data.

---

## Slide 6 — How the AI Is Actually Used

**Title**

`Where AI fits: graph-based prioritization, not AI magic`

**Bullets**

- Rules, feeds, and operational signals detect events first
- The graph connects IPs, services, accounts, suppliers, tenders, and campaigns
- The GNN ranks which linked entities deserve attention first
- Explanations, uncertainty, and trust checks are shown before action
- Cyber is the strongest live lane today, with fraud and corruption proving the architecture generalizes

**Speaker note**

The GNN is not the first detector and not the final judge. Rules catch exact patterns. The GNN helps decide whether a linked structure is actually risky in context. That is how we reduce noise and make the system operationally useful.

---

## Slide 7 — Live Proof and Operational Readiness

**Title**

`What the live system proves today`

**Bullets**

- Live cyber telemetry is ingested and correlated through the same operational workflow
- Current dashboard shows the platform is actively processing large event volume
- The investigate view shows live scored entities with evidence and trust posture
- The defense view shows bounded containment with delivery receipts
- Federation scenarios show how one actor pattern can touch multiple institutions

**Speaker note**

This is not a slide-only project. The strongest proof today is the live cyber lane plus bounded containment, then the federated cross-agency scenarios. That is enough to show the system is operationally real, not just conceptually interesting.

---

## Slide 8 — Trust, Ethics, and Public Leadership

**Title**

`Responsible AI is a core feature, not a disclaimer`

**Bullets**

- Outputs are explainable, auditable, and bounded
- Corruption outputs are framed as investigative risk, not legal proof
- Human review remains in the loop for sensitive decisions
- Pseudonymisation, scoped access, and audit trails reduce misuse risk
- The platform exposes caveats instead of hiding uncertainty

**Speaker note**

Public trust does not come from claiming the AI is always right. It comes from building a system that remains safe, inspectable, and accountable even when the model is uncertain. That is one of Sentinel-KE’s strongest leadership signals.

`Why this stands out from what is already in the market`

**Bullets**

**Title**

`A future-shaping direction for Kenya`

**Bullets**

- Kenya should not only digitize services; Kenya should also build sovereign systems to defend them
- Sentinel-KE shows a practical model for national digital defense across agencies and sectors
