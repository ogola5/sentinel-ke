## Presentation Structure

### First 5 minutes

Use the 10-slide deck only.

Target outcome:

- judges understand the national relevance
- judges understand the operational workflow
- judges see the strongest live evidence without being overloaded

Keep the verbal frame:

> "Sentinel-KE is a sovereign intelligence workflow: ingest, correlate, score, explain, contain, and report."

### Next 10 minutes

Use only the demo sequence in [DEMO_10MIN_RUNBOOK.md](/home/ogola/personal/sentinel-ke/docs/DEMO_10MIN_RUNBOOK.md):

- `Command`
- `Live Feed`
- `Threat Graph`
- `Investigate`
- `Defense`
- `Reports`

Do not improvise extra screens unless directly asked.

## Strongest Claims

Use these repeatedly:

- cyber lane is live and strongly evidenced
- fraud lane has a fresh reproducible PaySim benchmark artifact
- corruption lane is a risk-ranking and evidence lane, not legal proof
- containment works as a signed cross-system workflow
- agencies can integrate through connector APIs and legacy export bridges

## Claims To Avoid

- the GNN alone detects everything
- the model proves attribution
- corruption output is legal proof
- PaySim validates cyber
- the public cloud deployment is already complete if it is not
- the partner simulator is a production third-party partner

If time gets tight:

- cut depth
- do not cut honesty
- do not add new claims

This presentation wins by being operationally credible, not by sounding grander than the evidence.
